# strom-dsp test corpus: attribution

The clips of the strom-dsp test corpus (release asset `strom-dsp-corpus-v*.tar.gz`)
come from the sources below. Each is under CC0 1.0, in the public domain, or
under CC BY 3.0 / 4.0; the licences require the attribution given here.
Clips were cut, resampled to 48 kHz and in some cases processed (codec,
band-pass, gain and hard clipping, mixing); `manifest.tsv` records what was
done to each. Generated test signals are CC0.
